Delete your Discount account

Last updated: November 7, 2026

You can delete your account and personal data at any time. This page explains how.

Option 1: delete inside the app (fastest)

  • Open the Discount app and sign in
  • Tap the person icon on the Home screen to open Profile
  • Tap Delete my account, then confirm

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you can't open the app, email apoorvaj1405@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with. Use the subject "Delete my Discount account". We will delete your account within 7 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Your sign-in record with Firebase
  • Your shop credit balances
  • Your referral records

What may be kept

Payment records (shop, bill amount, discount and date) may be kept without any link to you, because shops and accounting rules need them.
