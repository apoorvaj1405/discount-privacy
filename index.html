Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Discount ("we", "our", "the app") helps people get discounts at partner shops in Bangalore. You scan a shop's UPI QR code, see the discount the shop offers, and pay the shop directly in your own UPI app. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and how you can control it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. What we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Email addressTo create your account, let you sign in, and send verification and password-reset emails
PasswordHandled by Firebase (Google). We never see or store your password
Payment records (shop, bill amount, discount, amount you paid, date)To show your savings and history, and so shops can be billed correctly
Shop credit balancesTo remember credit a shop has given you for your next visit at that shop
Referral activity (your code, and who signed up with it)To show your referral counts
Shop details (for partner shops): name, address, UPI ID, optional owner phoneTo list the shop and show its UPI ID so you can pay it

The camera is used only to scan QR codes on your device. We do not save photos or video. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or advertising ID.

2. Payments

You pay shops directly with your own UPI app. Discount does not process, hold or move your money. We record what you tell us you paid so we can show your savings.

3. How we use your data

  • To let you sign in securely
  • To calculate discounts and show your savings and history
  • To track shop credit and referral counts
  • To calculate the fee a shop owes us after a sale
  • To fix problems and keep the app working

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Your consent

By creating an account you consent to the collection and use of your data as described here. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see Section 7).

5. Who we share data with

We share data only with the services we need to run the app:

  • Firebase (Google) — for sign-in and for sending verification and password-reset emails
  • Our hosting and database providers — to run the app and store your account and payment records securely

These providers may use your data only to provide their service to us. Partner shops can see the payment records for their own shop.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account and records while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account, shop credit and referral records. Payment records may be kept without any link to you, because shops and accounting rules need them.

7. Your rights and how to delete your account

  • Access — ask for a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct wrong data
  • Deletion — in the app, open Profile and tap "Delete my account". Or see our account deletion page
  • Withdraw consent — delete your account at any time

8. Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our servers and with secure sign-in through Firebase. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

Discount is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
