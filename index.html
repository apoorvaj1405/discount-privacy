Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Discount ("we", "our", "the app") is a hyperlocal discount and cashback platform that connects users with local merchants in Bangalore. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and how you can control it, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. What we collect

DataWhy we collect it
Mobile numberTo verify your identity and let you log in (via OTP)
Transaction historyTo calculate discounts, track your savings, and show your history
Wallet balanceTo track savings earned and enable withdrawals
Referral activityTo credit referral bonuses correctly
Merchant UPI ID (for merchants)To process payments to the correct shop

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any data beyond what is listed above, unless you choose to provide it.

2. How we use your data

  • To verify your phone number and let you log in securely
  • To calculate and apply merchant discounts to your transactions
  • To credit savings and referral bonuses to your wallet
  • To process withdrawal requests
  • To improve the app and fix issues

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Your consent

By creating an account, you consent to the collection and use of your mobile number and transaction data as described in this policy. You may withdraw consent at any time by requesting account deletion (see Section 6).

4. Data sharing

We share limited data only where necessary to operate the app:

  • Firebase (Google) — for phone number verification (OTP)
  • Razorpay — for processing payments between you and merchants
  • Our database host — for securely storing your account and transaction data

These providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only to provide their service to us.

5. Data retention

We retain your account and transaction data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within a reasonable time, except where we are required to retain transaction records for legal or regulatory purposes.

6. Your rights

  • Access — you can request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — you can ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — you can request your account and data be deleted
  • Withdraw consent — you can stop using the app and request data erasure at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our servers, and secure storage of your information.

8. Children's data

Discount is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
